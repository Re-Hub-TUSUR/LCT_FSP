# Candidate scenarios

The rules: [profile](../business/brd/02-candidate-profile.md), [test](../business/brd/03-assessment.md), [invitations](../business/brd/04-invitations-chat.md), [vacancies](../business/brd/06-vacancies-tasks.md).

## UC-К-01 Uploading a file

**As** a candidate, **I want** to attach a PDF or DOCX, **so that** the form fills in and I check the fields before saving. Without a model key the form stays manual. FR-К-02.

## UC-К-02 Filling the form

**As** a candidate, **I want** to press «Сохранить резюме», **so that** the profile appears in the bank. Saving does not set a grade. FR-К-01, FR-К-03.

## UC-К-03 A second resume

**As** a candidate, **I want** to add a resume in another profession, **so that** it does not replace the first. FR-К-04.

## UC-К-04 A card without contacts

**As** a candidate, **I want** to know that email is hidden, **so that** the company sees it only after «Принять». FR-К-05.

## UC-К-05 Starting the test

**As** a candidate, **I want** to press «Начать тест», **so that** I pick a grade and receive tasks. FR-К-12.

## UC-К-06 Choosing a grade

**As** a candidate, **I want** to pick one of five grades, **so that** the test matches the level. FR-К-13.

## UC-К-07 Solving a task

**As** a candidate, **I want** to answer questions and run code, **so that** finishing sets a score. FR-К-14, FR-К-15.

## UC-К-08 Draft

**As** a candidate, **I want** to leave the page, **so that** the answer is not lost. FR-К-17.

## UC-К-09 Answers

**As** a candidate, **I want** to write answers and press «Отправить ответы», **so that** the grade is confirmed or not. FR-К-16.

## UC-К-10 Category

**As** a candidate, **I want** to see the specialization and grade after a counted attempt, **so that** the employer sees the confirmation. FR-К-18, FR-К-20.

## UC-К-11 The next grade

**As** a candidate with a high score, **I want** to open the test one level up, **so that** I do not wait to change an already confirmed grade. FR-К-19.

## UC-К-12 History

**As** a candidate, **I want** to see past attempts, **so that** I know the score and the status. FR-К-22.

## UC-К-13 Changing a grade

**As** a candidate, **I want** to press «Пройти тест на другой грейд», **so that** after the pause I change the confirmed level. FR-К-21.

## UC-К-14 Incoming

**As** a candidate, **I want** to open the invitation list, **so that** I see the company, the band, and the status. FR-К-23.

## UC-К-15 Accept

**As** a candidate, **I want** to press «Принять», **so that** contacts and the chat open. FR-К-24, FR-К-25, FR-О-07.

## UC-К-16 Decline

**As** a candidate, **I want** to press «Отклонить», **so that** the invitation closes without a chat. FR-К-25.

## UC-К-17 Answering a meeting

**As** a candidate, **I want** to confirm or decline a meeting. FR-О-09.

## UC-К-18 Take-home

**As** a candidate, **I want** to take or refuse a company's test, **so that** the platform grade does not change. FR-К-29.

## UC-К-19 Vacancy swipes

**As** a candidate, **I want** to swipe a vacancy, **so that** I mark interest or skip. FR-К-27.

## UC-К-20 Feed filters

**As** a candidate, **I want** to narrow the feed by salary, city, and stack and save the search. FR-К-26.

## UC-К-21 What the bank shows

**As** a candidate, **I want** the bank to show the stack and the test score, but not the email. FR-К-06.

## UC-К-22 Pause

**As** a candidate, **I want** to turn on «Не ищу работу», **so that** I leave the bank. FR-К-07.

## UC-К-23 FSP link

**As** a candidate, **I want** to press «Связать с ФСП», **so that** achievements appear on the card. FR-К-09, FR-К-10.

## UC-К-24 No history

**As** a candidate without results, **I want** to see that an empty registry is not an error and not a penalty. FR-К-11.

## UC-К-25 Unlink

**As** a candidate, **I want** to press «Отвязать», **so that** the imported results disappear. FR-К-09.
