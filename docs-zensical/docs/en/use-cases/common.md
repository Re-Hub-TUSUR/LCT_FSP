# Shared scenarios

## UC-О-01 Choosing a role

**As** a new person, **I want** to press «Я ищу работу» or «Я нанимаю», **so that** sign-up for that role opens. П-О-01.

## UC-О-02 Creating an account

**As** a new person, **I want** to enter email, a password, and consent, **so that** a code arrives by email. FR-О-01, FR-О-02, FR-К-08.

## UC-О-03 Confirmation

**As** a new person, **I want** to enter 6 digits, **so that** I can sign in. A wrong code does not open the cabinet. FR-О-01.

## UC-О-04 Sign-in

**As** a person with an account, **I want** to enter email and password, **so that** the cabinet of my role opens. An unconfirmed email asks for the code. FR-О-01.

## UC-О-05 Conversation

**As** a chat participant, **I want** to press «Отправить», **so that** the other person sees the message. A file over 10 MB does not go out. FR-О-08.

## UC-О-06 Meetings

**As** an employer, **I want** to schedule a meeting with a link, **so that** the candidate presses «Принять» or «Отклонить» on the card in the chat. The employer does not see those buttons in the chat. FR-О-09.

## UC-О-07 A live event

**As** a person with an open tab, **I want** to see a like or a new message without a reload. FR-О-10.

## UC-О-08 Someone else's object

**As** a person of the wrong role, **I want** to be refused, **so that** I do not open someone else's invitation or resume. FR-О-03.

## UC-О-09 Sign-in through Yandex

**As** a specialist or an employer, **I want** to press «Войти через Яндекс», **so that** a session opens without the code from the letter. On «Регистрация» the first and last name are already in the form, and the portrait is in «Фото». On «Вход» a new person is not created: «Сначала зарегистрируйтесь». FR-О-12. П-О-08.

## UC-О-10 A banned person

**As** a banned specialist or employer, **I want** to see only the window with the reason and the support address, **so that** I understand why sign-in is closed and can write if it is a mistake. FR-О-04, FR-А-05. П-О-09.
