# Employer scenarios

The rules: [search](../business/brd/05-employer-matching.md), [vacancies](../business/brd/06-vacancies-tasks.md).

## UC-Р-01 The first profile

**As** an employer, **I want** to save the company, **so that** an invitation can go out. Without a profile the system asks to fill it in. FR-Р-01.

## UC-Р-02 Swipes

**As** an employer, **I want** to press «Интересно» or «Пропустить», **so that** I mark a person in the deck. FR-Р-04.

## UC-Р-03 Bank

**As** an employer, **I want** to filter the feed, **so that** I see people of the right grade and city. FR-Р-02.

## UC-Р-04 Card before contact

**As** an employer, **I want** to open the card, **so that** I see the reasons and do not see the email. FR-Р-03.

## UC-Р-05 Sending

**As** an employer, **I want** to enter the text, the band, and a contact method and press «Отправить приглашение». FR-Р-05, FR-О-06.

## UC-Р-06 Without a vacancy

**As** an employer, **I want** to invite a person from the bank without publishing a vacancy. FR-Р-06.

## UC-Р-07 A repeat

**As** an employer, **I want** to be refused if the person has not answered the previous invitation. FR-Р-07.

## UC-Р-08 Statuses

**As** an employer, **I want** to see «Отправлено», «Принято», and «Отклонено». FR-Р-08, FR-О-05.

## UC-Р-09 Publishing

**As** an employer, **I want** to press «Опубликовать», **so that** the vacancy appears in the candidate feed. FR-Р-09.

## UC-Р-10 A match

**As** an employer, **I want** to answer the candidate's interest, **so that** a chat opens. FR-К-28.

## UC-Р-11 A meeting

**As** an employer, **I want** to schedule a meeting with a link. FR-О-09.

## UC-Р-12 A take-home

**As** an employer, **I want** to send a take-home, **so that** the score does not change the platform grade. FR-Р-10.

## UC-Р-13 An ATS poll

**As** an employer, **I want** to press «Подключить демо» and «Опросить», **so that** I see a decision on external applications and do not mix them into the bank. FR-Р-11.
