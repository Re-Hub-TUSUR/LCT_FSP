# Administrator scenarios

The rules: [administrator](../business/brd/07-admin.md).

## UC-А-01 Sign-in

**As** an administrator, **I want** to open the admin subdomain and enter a name and password in the browser window, **so that** I land in «Админка» at once. FR-А-01.

## UC-А-02 A new task

**As** an administrator, **I want** to press «Добавить задание», **so that** it enters the platform bank. FR-А-02.

## UC-А-03 A task snapshot

**As** an administrator, **I want** to open the task table, **so that** I see how they were solved. FR-А-03.

## UC-А-04 A company card

**As** an administrator, **I want** to confirm or refuse the company's documents. When the company deletes every file, the request is not in the list. In «Работодатели под подозрением» the company appears at once, with the «Сигналы» list, and the decision stays mine. FR-А-04. П-А-08.

## UC-А-05 A block

**As** an administrator, **I want** to block an email, a domain, or a company with a reason, **so that** both a specialist and an employer see only the window with that reason. The ban list shows whom the record hits. FR-А-05, FR-О-04.

## UC-А-06 Unblock

**As** an administrator, **I want** to lift the block and write a reason, **so that** it stays in the history. FR-А-05.

## UC-А-07 A dictionary

**As** an administrator, **I want** to add a specialization or a skill, or archive a value. FR-А-06.

## UC-А-08 A parameter

**As** an administrator, **I want** to change a parameter with a comment, **so that** the journal keeps the old and the new value. FR-А-07.

## UC-А-09 The journal

**As** an administrator, **I want** to open the journal and export CSV. FR-А-08.
