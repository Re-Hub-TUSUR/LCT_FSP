# Coverage

What the product does relative to the original brief. Behavior is specified in the [BRD](brd/index.md). This page does not promise anything the chapters do not contain.

| Area | Requirements | How it works |
|------|--------------|--------------|
| The employer reaches out | FR-Р-02, FR-Р-04, FR-Р-05, FR-К-23 | Bank, swipes, an invitation with a band |
| Category from the test, not the resume | FR-К-03, FR-К-13, FR-К-14, FR-К-20 | Five grades, an attempt from the bank |
| Contacts after the candidate agrees | FR-О-07, FR-К-25 | «Принять» opens email for that company |
| The band before the chat | FR-О-06 | «от» and «до» on the invitation and the vacancy |
| FSP | FR-К-09, FR-К-10, FR-К-11 | Link, import, an empty registry is not an error |
| Vacancies and mutual interest | FR-Р-09, FR-К-26, FR-К-27, FR-К-28 | Publishing, a like, a chat after mutual interest |
| Company take-home | FR-Р-10, FR-К-29 | An assignment for the candidate, the platform grade stays |
| ATS | FR-Р-11 | A poll, our filters, write-back, the bank does not change |
| Admin | FR-А-02, FR-А-03, FR-А-05, FR-А-06, FR-А-07, FR-А-08 | Task bank, a snapshot, ban list, dictionaries, journal |
| Sign-in through Yandex ID | FR-О-12 | «Войти через Яндекс», the email is confirmed, the name and portrait are in the form |
